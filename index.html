A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Getting started with dawvq.

What is dawvq?

dawvq brings your AI model access, API keys, wallet and request history into one workspace. Use the panel to manage your account and connect your tools.

Can I use my existing account?

Yes. Sign in with your existing credentials. If your account needs a credentials update, the workspace will guide you through it.

Which models can I use?

Your workspace shows the current model catalog. Availability depends on your account, the selected API key and provider availability.

How do top-ups work?

Open Wallet in your workspace and follow the checkout instructions. Check the asset, network, amount and expiry before sending a payment. Your balance is credited after payment confirmation and matching.

Where can I find connection instructions?

Open Setup in your workspace for client configuration, API connection details and CLI instructions.

How do I contact support?

Select Support and sign in to open your tickets. You can create a ticket, read replies and continue the conversation from your workspace.
