Crypto wallet top-up

After signing in, choose an available cryptocurrency and network, enter a USD amount, and receive the exact payment address and QR code. The checkout shows the amount, network and expiry before you send funds.

Verified delivery

Funds are credited only after the payment is confirmed and matched to the order. Partial, unsigned or mismatched payments are not credited automatically.

How it works

  1. Create an account with a login and password, or sign in to your existing account.
  2. Open Wallet in your panel and select Add Tokens.
  3. Select the cryptocurrency and follow the instructions.
  4. Wait for network confirmation; your API balance updates automatically.
